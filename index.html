§ White Paper · 01

Methodology

How we compute your ideological coordinates across three compasses and match you against 100+ registered South African parties.

The Three Compasses

SA Compass: Transformation ↔ Stability / State Authority ↔ Constitutional Liberalism. Built specifically for South Africa's political reality.

Global Compass: Economic Left ↔ Right / Authoritarian ↔ Libertarian. Classic political science model.

Ideological Map: Your position within a full taxonomy from Stalinism to Anarcho-Capitalism, with historical context and South African manifestations.

Party Scoring

Each of the 100+ parties has been assigned a position on all eight primary axes using 2024 election manifestos, Parliamentary Monitoring Group voting records, and Zondo Commission testimony. Alignment is computed as Euclidean distance in 8-dimensional space.

We also score parties on governance effectiveness, capital tier, and coalition compatibility — dimensions that pure policy matching misses.

Five Outcome Tiers
Tier 1 — Clear Match
Strong alignment with one party. Full critical dossier: history, leadership, successes, scandals, coalition notes, and an honest “why vote / why not” verdict.
Tier 2 — Spread Alignment
Swing voter. Multiple parties above 50%. Analysis of your centre position and what it means in the current coalition landscape.
Tier 3 — No Meaningful Alignment
Your views aren't represented by any current party. Your named ideology, plus what a party for you would require.
Tier 4 — Constitutional Ideal
Your position mirrors the constitutional compromise — and the gap between that mandate and what parties actually deliver.
Tier 5 — Hyper-Local Fit
Your best match is a civic/local interest block. National parties have failed you; your vote may matter most at ward level.
Limitations — Stated Openly

Party positions shift. Manifestos are aspirational, not operational. Our scoring reflects stated positions, not governance outcomes. Smaller parties may have less profiling depth, but we aim for equal rigour across all 100+.

VERITAS is advisory and experimental. It cannot decode your vote for you. Your vote belongs to you — not to this instrument. Sources for every party position are linked from each party dossier.

§ 01

Mandate

A truth engine for the citizen. We map personal conviction onto parliamentary reality — across over 100 registered parties, not just the loudest six.

VERITAS operates as a transparent bridge between the voter and the manifesto. By distilling complex legislative positions into a precise multi-axis policy matrix, we enable South Africans to see beyond partisan rhetoric and into the mechanical heart of governance.

Every data point is anchored in recorded parliamentary votes, official policy papers, published manifestos, and constitutional mandates. Our party profiles are grouped by systemic influence, capital tier, operational agent, ideological neutrality, governance effectiveness, and strategic positioning — dimensions that pure policy matching misses.

VERITAS does not tell you how to vote. It shows you where you stand — and then gives you the evidence to decide what to do with that information.

§ 02

Why This Exists

South Africa's democratic participation faces a crisis not of apathy alone, but of information. Voters choose parties based on historical loyalty, personality, ethnicity, and inherited allegiance — not policy alignment. The result is a Parliament that frequently does not reflect the actual policy preferences of the people who elect it.

The "popular personality" problem is real. Leaders become brands. Parties become tribes. Manifestos go unread. Policy debate is replaced by spectacle.

VERITAS is built on a single premise: an informed voter is a more sovereign voter. When you know where you stand on land reform, the NHI, foreign policy, and constitutional governance — and which of the 100+ parties actually match that stance — your vote becomes a deliberate act rather than a habitual one.

This does not mean your historical loyalties are wrong. It means you can examine them.

§ 03

Procedure

01
Articulate Your Stance
Respond to policy prompts drawn from current parliamentary debate — land, NHI, BEE, energy, immigration, foreign policy, governance, and more. Each question includes a neutral context brief. No prior political knowledge required. Choose your depth:
  • Quick (20 questions) — A directional first read. Not a verdict.
  • Standard (45 questions) — The recommended mode. Reliable for most users.
  • Deep Profile (80+ questions) — The most accurate version. Compass placement and ideology identification are significantly more precise.
02
Synthesize Your Ideology
Our engine maps your responses onto three compasses simultaneously:
  • The SA Compass — Transformation ↔ Stability / State Authority ↔ Constitutional Liberalism. Built specifically for South Africa's political reality.
  • The Global Compass — Classic economic left/right × authoritarian/libertarian. Situates you against international political movements.
  • The Full Ideological Map — From Stalinism to Anarcho-Capitalism. Identifies your nearest named ideology and explains it honestly, including its flaws and historical record.
03
Align Your Representation
Discover which of the 100+ registered parties mirror your policy stance — with transparent per-axis match percentages and full party dossiers covering history, ideology, leadership background, parliamentary record, scandals, weaknesses, delivery track record, and coalition compatibility. We do not flatten the picture. The uncomfortable facts are included.
§ 04

Editorial Pledge

"This instrument is advisory.
Your vote is a sacred article of democratic participation.
Let no algorithm decode it for you."
— VERITAS Charter, Clause 1
We do not collect your data.

Your responses never leave your device. No server receives your answers. No profile is built. No analytics track your political position. This is a constitutional instrument, not a data product. POPIA compliant by design — there is nothing to protect because nothing is collected.

We do not endorse any party.

Every party profile — from the ANC to the smallest civic movement — is written to the same critical standard. Successes and failures documented with equal rigour. Sources cited. Where our characterisation may be disputed, we say so explicitly and link to the primary source.

We do not replace the manifesto.

VERITAS synthesizes public record. It is a starting point, not a conclusion. We link directly to every party's official manifesto from their dossier page. Read them before you vote.

Preamble — Adopted 2026

VERITAS Charter

We the builders of this instrument acknowledge:

  • That South Africa's democracy is thirty years old and under strain.
  • That the vote is the most powerful instrument an ordinary citizen possesses.
  • That information asymmetry between the governed and the governing is itself a form of disenfranchisement.
  • That no algorithm, no compass, no quiz can tell a South African how to exercise their constitutional right.
  • That this tool exists only to illuminate — never to direct.
  • That we may be wrong, and that we remain open to correction.

VERITAS is advisory. Experimental. Imperfect. It is offered in the spirit of Section 19 of the Constitution:

"Every adult citizen has the right to vote in elections for any legislative body established in terms of this Constitution, and to do so in secret; and every adult citizen has the right to stand for public office and, if elected, to hold office." — Section 19, Constitution of the Republic of South Africa, 1996

That right belongs to you. Not to this instrument. Use it accordingly.
