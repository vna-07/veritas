Methodology

How we compute your ideological coordinates, match you against 100+ parties, and why nothing about the calculation is hidden from you.

The Question Bank

Our questionnaire draws from 265 single‑concept policy statements spanning 20 thematic categories, from Land & Property to Constitutional Law. Each question is anchored in current South African parliamentary debate and includes a neutral context brief. No prior political knowledge is required.

Every question is pre‑tagged with a directional weight on eight primary axes, described below. When you answer a question on a 5‑point Likert scale (Strongly Disagree to Strongly Agree), your response multiplies the axis weight, creating your personal political vector.

An optional confidence weighting allows you to indicate how strongly you hold a view. Uncertain responses are weighted proportionally less.

The Axes

SA‑Specific Compass (Compass 1)

Transformation ↔ Stability: Measures the appetite for structural change versus preserving existing institutions.
State Authority ↔ Constitutional Liberalism: Centralised state power versus strict constitutional limits and separation of powers.
Ubuntu Collectivism ↔ Individual Rights: Communal, collective responsibility versus individual autonomy.
Pan‑African ↔ Western Alignment: Alignment with African continental interests versus Western institutions and values.
Traditional Authority ↔ Secular Democracy: Deference to traditional leaders and cultural practices versus secular, elected governance.
Racial Redress ↔ Non‑Racialism: Race‑conscious transformation policies versus race‑neutral, colour‑blind approaches.

Global Standard Compass (Compass 2)

Economic Left ↔ Economic Right: State‑driven planning and redistribution versus free markets and private enterprise.
Authoritarian ↔ Libertarian: State social control and enforcement versus absolute individual liberty.

Party Matching

Each of the 100+ registered political parties has been assigned a position on all eight axes. These positions are derived from their 2024 election manifestos, parliamentary voting records (via the Parliamentary Monitoring Group), on‑record leadership statements, and Zondo Commission testimony where relevant.

We compare your axis vector to each party's vector using Euclidean distance in 8‑dimensional space. The similarity percentage is calculated as (1 − distance / max_distance) × 100.

Beyond pure policy matching, we also categorise parties on a multi‑dimensional strategic taxonomy, including their systemic influence, capital tier, ideological neutrality, and governance effectiveness. This allows you to see not just who aligns with your views, but who has the capacity and track record to implement them in the real world of South African governance.

Outcome Tiers

Tier 1 — Clear Match
≥70% similarity with one party, ≥10% lead over second. You receive a full critical dossier.
Tier 2 — Spread Alignment
3+ parties above 50%, no clear leader. You are a pragmatic swing voter.
Tier 3 — No Meaningful Alignment
Top match below 50%. Your views are unrepresented. We name your ideology and what a party for you would need.
Tier 4 — Constitutional Ideal
Very low spread across all parties. Your position mirrors the constitutional compromise — and the gap between that mandate and what parties deliver.
Tier 5 — Hyper‑Local Fit
Your best match is a civic/local interest block. Your vote may matter most at ward level.

Data Sources

All data is anchored in public, verifiable records: IEC electoral results (1994–2024), Parliamentary Monitoring Group voting records, Zondo State Capture Commission final report (2022), individual party manifestos (2024), and the Constitution of the Republic of South Africa (Act 108 of 1996).

Limitations — Stated Openly

Party positions shift. Manifestos are aspirational. Governments do not always implement them. Our scoring reflects stated positions, not governance outcomes.

No algorithm captures full political identity. Your result is a data point, not a definition. You are more than your compass coordinates.

Smaller parties receive less profiling depth. The top 40 parties receive full dossiers. The remaining civic and local blocks receive standardised profiles reflecting their electoral weight.

VERITAS is advisory and experimental. Offered in the spirit of Section 19 of the Constitution. Your vote belongs to you — not to this instrument.
